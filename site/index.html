Hostalorgs

Two forts, two keys. Grab the enemy key from their basement vault and run it up to the turn-in on your own roof.

Play now →

Runs in your browser: no download, no account. Best on a desktop with keyboard and mouse in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Teams

Red against Blue. Pick a side or let the game balance you.

Scoring

One point per key carried home. The carrier glows in the key's colour.

Practice

Defender bots hold each fort, so a solo player has something to get past.

Six classes

  • ScoutFastest. Nailgun, concussion and flash grenades.
  • SoldierRocket launcher, heavy armour.
  • SniperCharged rifle with a scope, auto-rifle.
  • DemolitionsGrenade launcher with sticky charges.
  • HeavyRotary cannon. Slow, very hard to stop.
  • MedicHeals the team to 150, infects enemies.
